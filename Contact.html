Pragnya Women's Degree College details

Pragnya Women's Degree College, Hyderabad, is a prestigious autonomous institution established in 2015 and affiliated with Osmania University. It offers comprehensive undergraduate and postgraduate programs across Science, Commerce, and Arts disciplines. The college holds a high accreditation with a NAAC "A+" grade and is recognized by UGC and AICTE. Located centrally near Chandanagar, it has a reputation for quality education and offers courses such as B.Sc, B.Com, BA, M.Sc, MA, and M.Com with admissions primarily conducted via the Telangana DOST portal for UG and TS CPGET for PG courses. The college has excellent infrastructure facilities like hostels, canteen, library, labs, and sports complex.
