About Our College

Welcome to our College,Pragnya Women's Degree College is a place where students learn and grow.
It provides a good learning environment with helpful lecturers.
The college offers quality education and encourages students to develop their skills.
It helps students build confidence and achieve their goals.

History of our College

Pragnya Women's Degree College was established in the year 2011 at Chanda Nagar, Hyderabad. The college was started with the aim of providing quality education to women. It offers various undergraduate courses and focuses on the academic and personal development of students. The college is affiliated with Osmania University and continues to support students in achieving their goals.
